## Team

- Teams of **1 to 6 people**.
- Every member must be registered, accepted for the **same Málaga Local Event**
  and joined to the team on the platform. Being physically at the venue does not
  replace registration.
- A **Team Owner** manages team creation/configuration and coordinates Project
  editing. The Team Owner does not have to lead all the work.
- Participants under 18 require registration by a parent/legal guardian and
  accompaniment during the event.

## Before development starts

- **Allowed before Saturday 09:00:** meeting people, forming teams, reading the
  complete challenges and getting familiar with resources and tools.
- **Not allowed:** advancing the development of the solution. The solution is
  built during the hackathon. Pre-existing materials or work must be declared.

## Deliverables: Project page

The Project page is the record the jury reviews. The demo is only one part of the
submission.

| Element | What it must contain |
| --- | --- |
| Name and summary | Project name + 50–200 word summary: what was built, how it answers the challenge and why it matters |
| Project Demo | A single public link: presentation of at most **7 slides including the cover**, OR video of at most **30 seconds** |
| Final Project | Public link to the project, repository, site or material that allows the solution to be understood and reviewed |
| Detailed description | What it does, how it works, benefits, tools, method, real development status and limitations |
| NASA Data | NASA resources used, links and explanation of their contribution. For Global Awards: at least one NASA resource from the challenge |
| Other data and references | Partner-agency sources and references for data, code, images, text and tools, even when open |
| AI / SI use | Tools used and how they were incorporated; required labels/declarations for generated content |
| Review and acceptance | All required fields and checkboxes, correct members, accessible links, submission confirmed within the deadline |

**Language and access:** page, slides and written materials in **English**. A video
may be narrated in another language only with **English subtitles**. Demo and Final
Project links must open **without password, permission, registration or access
request**.

## Project Demo rules

### Option A: slides (maximum 7, cover included)

Suggested structure:

1. Team and project name · challenge chosen · one sentence explaining the solution. Do not identify minors.
2. Concrete problem and the people or context affected.
3. Solution and user journey, with a simple diagram.
4. NASA data or resource: origin and real role in the project.
5. Demonstration of the prototype, result or evidence of the concept.
6. What has been verified, what works and its limitations.
7. Expected impact, next step and essential references.

The demo must be understandable without a live explanation, legible, honest
(built vs simulated vs planned) and accessible.

### Option B: video (maximum 30 seconds)

Recommended four-beat script:

1. **Problem** — concrete problem and who/what it affects.
2. **Solution** — what was built and the main idea.
3. **Demo** — a visible working part, a result or evidence of the concept.
4. **NASA** — which NASA resource is used and what value it adds.

Technical checks: duration (no more than 30 s), audio clarity, English subtitles
if narration is not in English, public access without login, and no identifiable
names, voices or images of minors.

## Submission

- Drafts saved with **Save Progress**, sharing links or uploading files to Drive
  are useful steps, but the project is only submitted when **Submit for
  Judging** is completed and the confirmation is shown.
- **Deadline:** Sunday 15 November 2026, before 23:59 in the local event time
  zone. No later edits or exceptions are accepted for global evaluation.
- If any content changes before the deadline: save and **submit again**, then
  verify that the final version was submitted.
- If the platform fails: capture the exact message, time, team URL and steps that
  triggered the error; hide personal data and do not share passwords. Support does
  not guarantee a deadline extension.
- The local Drive space provided by the organization does **not** replace the
  Project page plus Submit for Judging.
- Submitting within the deadline gives the participation certificate (per the
  official 2026 page).

## Responsible use

- **AI is allowed.** The team remains responsible for decisions, verification and
  authorship of the submitted content.
- Declare AI/SI tools used and how they were incorporated.
- Add visible marking to generated images and videos; recognize generated code
  and data in the description/metadata.
- Do not use NASA brand elements (logos, flags, mission identifiers) in generated
  content.
- Do not upload credentials, unnecessary personal data or confidential material.
- Cite external materials and check their rights and licenses; do not assume reuse
  rights just because material is accessible online.
- **Minors' privacy:** no identifiable names, voices or images of people under 18
  in the materials sent to NASA. Use screen recordings, the prototype itself or
  permitted illustrations instead.
- Submitting implies publishing the solution under the participation terms.

## Judging criteria

Reference criteria used by the jury (scale of 1 to 5 per criterion; percentages
are not fixed in this guide and the local rubric must be confirmed with the
organization):

| Criterion | What is valued |
| --- | --- |
| Impact | Problem, audience and quality/scope of the possible benefit; evidence supporting it |
| Creativity | Originality of the approach or significant improvement over alternatives |
| Validity | Scientific soundness and technical, functional or logical viability; evidence and limitations |
| Relevance | Answer to the challenge and substantial integration of NASA resources |
| Presentation | Clarity, structure and effectiveness when communicating problem, solution and importance |

Not assumed: an incomplete project is not automatically excluded; an app or code
is not a universal requirement; AI use is not penalized by itself; good staging
does not by itself prove validity, impact or relevance.

## Local pitch (Málaga)

- The **local pitch is not the Project Demo** and does not replace Submit for
  Judging.
- Expected on Sunday 15 November: technical tests 16:30–17:15 and pitches
  17:30–19:00.
- Oral presentation may be in Spanish or English; visual material and slides must
  be in **English**.
- Published references differ on pitch duration, slide limit and Q&A (2–2:30 min
  or 2–3 min, with 2–3 questions). Confirm the final rule with the organization
  before the technical test.

Recommended story: problem → solution → NASA resource → evidence → limits →
next step. Rehearse short answers supported by a screen, data point, test or
concrete decision.
