## Notes

- The official participant guide and the organization's announcements take
  precedence over the English summaries in this repository.
- The Drive files are **linked, not copied** into this repository: it is public,
  the files are binary/large, and NASA brand-restricted material should not be
  redistributed here.
- Check the Drive folders for updates before the submission deadline.
